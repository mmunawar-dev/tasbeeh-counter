1. Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

To provide accurate astronomical prayer schedules, Qibla compass alignment, and reliable Athan audio alerts, the App requests specific device permissions. We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization—only requesting permissions strictly necessary for user-requested religious utilities.

Permission Classification Exact Purpose in the App
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION 		Location Used in the foreground to calculate the accurate geographical bearing to the Kaaba in Makkah (Qibla Direction), compute precise local prayer times based on solar coordinates, and automatically detect your city/timezone. Location coordinates are processed on your device and are never stored or tracked on MH Studio servers.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Enables the App to deliver timely daily prayer alerts, Athan reminders, and worship notifications according to the preferences you configure (Sound, Vibration, Silent, or Off).
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
USE_EXACT_ALARM 		Exact Alarms Required under Google Play's Exact Alarm Policy to trigger Athan calls precisely at the calculated astronomical prayer minute, ensuring prayer times are strictly punctual.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Event Reschedules your upcoming daily prayer alarms automatically after your device restarts or when system time/timezone changes occur.
WAKE_LOCK System Utility Briefly wakes the device processor for a few milliseconds at the exact prayer time to display the prayer notification banner and play the selected reminder tone.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK 		Media Playback Powers continuous audio recitation of the 99 Names of Allah (Asma-ul-Husna). Displays a system media notification with Play, Pause, and Stop controls so you can listen while your screen is locked or while using other apps.
VIBRATE Haptic Feedback Provides tactile haptic vibrations when tapping the digital Tasbeeh counter bead and for silent/vibration prayer alerts.
INTERNET Network Access Connects to verified prayer calculation services (Aladhan API) via encrypted HTTPS, verifies in-app subscription licenses, and checks crash telemetry.
Manual Location Alternative: If you prefer not to grant GPS location access, you can choose any city worldwide from our offline database of popular cities. The App will accurately compute prayer times and Qibla bearing without accessing your device's GPS hardware.

2. How Data is Handled & Local Storage

No Account Required

The App does not require user registration, email sign-up, passwords, or personal profiles. You can access all worship features immediately and anonymously upon installation.

Device Sensors (Compass & Magnetometer)

The Qibla Direction feature accesses your phone's built-in magnetic field sensor (magnetometer) and accelerometer to calculate phone heading in real time. Sensor data is processed dynamically in your device's memory and is discarded immediately after display. It is never logged, stored, or transmitted over the internet.

Locally Stored Preferences (SharedPreferences)

The App stores your personal configuration exclusively on your device's private sandbox storage:

  • Selected Madhab (Hanafi, Shafi'i / Standard) & Fiqh Calculation Method (MWL, ISNA, Karachi, Makkah, Egypt, Tehran, etc.)
  • Selected City / Location coordinates and manual prayer time minute adjustments (+/- minutes)
  • Notification delivery modes per prayer (Sound, Vibration, Silent, Off)
  • Digital Tasbeeh presets, custom targets, and counter click modes
  • Bookmarked favorite supplications (Duas)
  • Interface preferences (Theme mode: Light/Dark/System and App Language)

This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to MH Studio servers.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

To provide accurate Islamic timings, application stability, and optional advertising support, the App utilizes trusted third-party service providers. These services operate under strict confidentiality and industry-standard security practices.

Aladhan Prayer Times API

The App connects to the Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com) via encrypted HTTPS to synchronize real-time astronomical prayer schedules. The request transmits your selected city name or approximate coordinates solely to compute prayer schedules. No personal identifiers, advertising IDs, or user credentials are sent to Aladhan.

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Google LLC)

To ensure high performance, monitor bugs, and maintain app stability across thousands of Android device models, the App may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These tools collect non-identifiable technical diagnostic data, including:

  • Device model, operating system version, and system language
  • Stack traces and memory dumps during application crashes
  • Anonymous feature interaction metrics

Review Google Firebase Privacy Policy →

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

The free version of the App may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect non-sensitive device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse IP address, and advertising interaction metrics to display relevant ads, enforce frequency capping, and prevent ad fraud. Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time through their device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because MH Studio does not maintain backend user accounts or central databases containing your information:

  • Local Preference Deletion: You can completely erase all saved locations, custom prayer time offsets, favorite Duas, and Tasbeeh counters at any time by going to your phone's Settings → Apps → Deen → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored sandboxed preferences from your device.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke Location, Notification, or Exact Alarm permissions at any time via your device's system settings without breaking the core offline Islamic content of the App.

7. Data Security & Transmission

We are committed to maintaining the security of your interactions with the App:

  • All outbound network communications (such as prayer time synchronization with Aladhan and crash reporting) use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption).
  • Local application storage is protected by Android's internal application sandboxing, preventing other unauthorized apps on your device from accessing your prayer settings.
  • While we employ robust security practices, no method of electronic storage or network transmission is guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.
